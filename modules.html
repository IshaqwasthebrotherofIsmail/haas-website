Training & Development

From first draw to competition ready.

A progressive pathway designed to build safe, capable and confident horse archers — from foundational archery to mounted competition.

Proposed assessment framework. These modules, grades and competition qualifications are a working HAA(S) proposal, not an officially adopted grading system. Assessment criteria, pass marks and recognition remain subject to Association approval and applicable competition rules.

Grading pathway

GRADE 1 · FOUNDATION

Ground Archer

Build safe handling and sound shooting habits before progressing to horses.

  • Range commands, equipment checks and safe retrieval
  • Stance, nocking, draw, release and follow-through
  • Controlled grouping at an introductory distance
  • Basic horse awareness and stable safety
Assessment: safety demonstration + ground shooting
GRADE 2 · HORSEMANSHIP

Mounted Foundations

Develop independent balance and calm, responsible handling around horses.

  • Mounting, dismounting and tack checks
  • Walk and trot control with instructor oversight
  • Independent seat, rein management and emergency stops
  • Dry-draw technique while stationary or led, where appropriate
Assessment: riding skills + safety check
GRADE 3 · INTEGRATION

Novice Horse Archer

Bring archery and horsemanship together on a controlled course.

  • Safe arrow handling and shooting position while mounted
  • Consistent course entry, line discipline and exit
  • Accurate shooting at a suitable supervised pace
  • Horse welfare and readiness checks
Assessment: supervised mounted run
GRADE 4 · PERFORMANCE

Intermediate Horse Archer

Demonstrate repeatable technique across more demanding courses.

  • Controlled riding and shooting at progressively higher pace
  • Multiple targets and direction changes where suitable
  • Consistent timing, accuracy and equipment management
  • Sportsmanship and independent course preparation
Assessment: scored multi-run practical
GRADE 5 · COMPETITION

Advanced Horse Archer

Show competition-level consistency, judgment and responsibility.

  • Performance across recognised course formats
  • Consistent safe execution under competition conditions
  • Horse welfare, rules knowledge and incident response
  • Mentoring and exemplary sporting conduct
Assessment: advanced practical + rules review

Competition qualification pathway

STAGE A

Club Practice Eligible

Complete foundational safety checks and obtain instructor clearance for the relevant activity. Riding and shooting permissions are assessed separately.

STAGE B

Novice Event Eligible

Pass the mounted safety assessment, demonstrate a controlled full course, and satisfy the organiser's novice-event rules and horse suitability requirements.

STAGE C

Open Competition Eligible

Demonstrate consistent scored runs, course-rule knowledge and instructor/assessor endorsement. Entry remains subject to event-specific eligibility rules.

STAGE D

Representative Selection

Consider recent verified results, consistency, conduct, fitness, horse welfare and selection criteria set by HAA(S) for each event.

How assessment could work

Each grade should include a mandatory safety pass, a practical skills checklist and a recorded assessment signed by an authorised assessor. A serious safety breach would stop an assessment regardless of shooting score. Scoring thresholds, distances, gaits, reassessment intervals and assessor accreditation should be formally approved before the system is launched.

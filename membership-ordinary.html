Ordinary Member

For individuals who want full participation in the Society, including a voice in its governance.

Who may apply

  • Any person interested in and supportive of the Society's objectives and activities may apply.
  • Submit an application in writing using the form prescribed by the Management Committee.
  • Admission requires Management Committee approval; submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance.

Rights and participation

  • An Ordinary Member in Good Standing may attend, speak and vote at General Meetings.
  • Each Ordinary Member has one individual vote.
  • Ordinary Members in Good Standing are eligible to hold office in the Society, subject to the Constitution's election and appointment provisions.

What Good Standing requires

  • Be a Financial Member: all subscriptions, fees and levies due must be fully paid.
  • Not be under suspension or subject to a disciplinary sanction.
  • Comply with the Constitution, by-laws, rules and resolutions of the Society.

Commitments and fees

  • Pay the prescribed annual subscription; the applicable rate is determined through the Society's governance processes.
  • Observe the Society's Constitution, rules and codes of conduct.
  • Support safe, respectful participation in Society activities. This is an expectation of conduct, not a fixed attendance quota.

General membership conditions

Members must follow the HAA(S) Constitution. Resignation requires one month's written notice to the Secretary or Membership Officer, and outstanding amounts remain payable. Serious misconduct or breaches may lead to disciplinary action under the Constitution, with an opportunity to respond.

Exact fee amounts, programme availability, and any additional administrative requirements should be confirmed with the Association; the Constitution does not set a fixed fee for every membership category.

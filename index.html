HORSE ARCHERY ASSOCIATION (SINGAPORE)

Building the sport. Preserving the art.

HAA(S) promotes, develops and preserves Horseback Archery and Traditional Archery in Singapore, creating structured opportunities for training, recreation and competition in a safe and supportive environment.

We foster skill, confidence, discipline and sportsmanship while building partnerships with communities and organisations that share our commitment to equestrian culture and traditional martial arts.

THE DISCIPLINE

OUR SYMBOL

Singaporean identity. International standards.

The Association’s emblem reflects its national identity, sporting excellence, and core values. The five stars represent its commitment to Singapore, while their colours, drawn from the standard World Archery target, signify adherence to international standards. The horseshoe symbolises strength, loyalty, resilience, and the Association’s equestrian heritage, while the Lionhead embodies national pride and Singapore’s fighting spirit. The two bows represent unity and camaraderie, with their downward low-guard stance reflecting the principles of restraint, tolerance, safety, trust, and the defensive nature of traditional martial arts.
